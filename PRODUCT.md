# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: solo tradies and small business owners in Greater Sydney, 1-10 staff, no dedicated IT or marketing person. They wear every hat, want things explained plainly (no jargon), are price sensitive but not cheap-seeking (they will pay a fair fixed price for real work). They are evaluating whether to trust one small provider with IT, their website, or automation instead of hiring in-house or juggling multiple vendors.

## Product Purpose

Coralstone Services Group sells three services to small business: AI Automation, IT Support, and Websites and SEO. Flagship product is Missed-Call Rescue ($497 setup + $79/mo). Success for a visitor is booking the free audit (the only sitewide CTA, via existing Calendly link).

## Positioning

Enterprise-grade engineer at small-business price. Founder is a Microsoft-certified (MS-102, MD-102) Modern Workplace and End User Compute engineer who managed device fleets and security for large Australian organisations, now applying the same standards (security, backups, migrations) to businesses that have never had access to them, at fixed fair prices with no lock-in. A competitor without that enterprise engineering background could not honestly copy this claim.

## Operating Context

Static-export Next.js site (no server runtime, no API routes) deployed on Vercel. Any dynamic behaviour is client-side JS calling external endpoints (Supabase Edge Functions). Only sitewide CTA is the free audit via Calendly. Contact form posts to Web3Forms. Content must read as real static HTML for search and AI crawlers, not a client-rendered shell.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Three service verticals: AI Automation, IT Support, Websites and SEO (SEO/AEO/GEO).
- Pricing: services with no listed price end with "We assess this in your free audit." AEO/AI-search readiness is a benefit of how sites are built, never priced separately, never guarantees rankings or AI visibility.
- Core brand constraint: month-to-month, no lock-in, on every relevant page.
- No claim states a specific result (numbers, rankings, revenue) unless from a named real deployment; directional benefits only otherwise.
- Never describes anything as fixed or improved relative to a past version of this site; present tense only.
- Plain ASCII copy, Australian English spelling, plain-English headlines with technical terms as small tags under cards.

## Product Principles

1. Trust is earned through the founder's real enterprise credential and plain-spoken fixed pricing, not through invented scale or authority.
2. One accountable person/provider replaces the fragmented small-business experience of juggling separate IT, web, and marketing vendors.
3. No lock-in and fixed pricing are structural promises, not marketing lines; every surface must be consistent with them.
4. Claims stay honest and present-tense: real client work can be shown, but no fabricated results, testimonials, or rankings.
5. The site must perform and read as genuine static content for both human visitors and AI/search crawlers.
